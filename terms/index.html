Last updated: September 2026

This website

This site exists to show real examples of our carpentry, decking and fencing work, and to make it easy to get in touch. It's informational, there's nothing to purchase, book, or sign up for directly through it. All the photos and video shown are of real work Neville has done for real clients on this coast.

Getting in touch

Clicking "Call" or "WhatsApp" anywhere on this site opens your own phone or WhatsApp app to contact us directly, it doesn't submit anything through this website. Any quote, scope of work, timeline, or price discussed is agreed directly between you and Neville, not through this site.

The actual work

Decking, pergolas, staircases and fencing work is scoped and agreed on a job-by-job basis after a real site visit, that's genuinely how we work, not just a line on this page. Questions
