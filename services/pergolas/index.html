Straight talk

How we approach a pergola

  1. 01

    Structure first, looks second

    A pergola has to stand up to the coast as well as look good. Every one is designed around structural integrity, with the frame sized for the job and the site.

  2. 02

    The right materials for the job

    Pergolas are built in garapa, balau or treated pine. Material selection and sourcing is part of the service, and Neville helps choose the timber that suits the structure, how exposed the spot is and the budget.

  3. 03

    Built to suit the house

    Recent builds include pergolas attached to the house, freestanding ones, an upper level pergola, pergolas over decks and pergolas with built-in seating. Each is designed for the property, not taken off a template.

  4. 04

    Measured on site

    Every project starts with a free site visit and honest measurements, followed by a straightforward quote.
