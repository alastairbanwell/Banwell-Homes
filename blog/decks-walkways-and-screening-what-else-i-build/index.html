Decks, Walkways And Screening: What Else I Build In Timber.

Most calls are about a deck. The same timber work also covers walkways, privacy screens and fences, and each has its own questions. Here is what the work includes in Betty's Bay.

Decking and walkways

A deck is the job most people think of first, but the work I do in Betty's Bay, Rooi Els, Kleinmond and Pringle Bay covers a bit more than that.

Decks and walkways

The decking side of the work is hardwood and treated softwood decks, wooden walkways and screening, all built to cope with the coastal climate. A walkway is built with the same timbers as a deck, and every job ends with a full site cleanup. The longer guide to choosing the timber is in choosing the right timber for decking on the Betty's Bay coast.

The timbers I use

  • Garapa, a hardwood.
  • Balau, also a hardwood.
  • Treated pine, a treated softwood, which suits most residential decks when it is properly treated for coastal use and maintained.

Hardwood costs more upfront, is denser and more rot resistant, and usually needs less frequent attention. For a deck in full sun and salt all day, hardwood is often the better long term investment. For fences, I work in treated pine.

Fences and screens

The fencing work runs from standard security fencing to decorative privacy screening. The types I build are:

  • Dropper fencing
  • Wire fencing
  • Wooden panel fencing
  • Privacy screening

Each is built for the property, not off a template.

Why a gap can be the better call

A fully closed privacy fence gives the wind a flat surface to push against. The taller and more solid it is, the more load goes through every post, so the posts and footings have to be sized for it. A slatted or gapped screen still blocks the view at eye level while letting some wind through. On an exposed property the posts go deeper and closer together, and I say so upfront. The full guide is in building a fence that holds up to Betty's Bay wind.

Getting started

Every quote starts with a free site visit, with no charge and no obligation, and I oversee every job myself from that first visit to the final cleanup. There is more in what happens at the site visit. If you are not sure whether you want a deck, a walkway or a screen, bring that to the visit and we will talk it through on the spot.

If you are planning a deck, a staircase or a fence, call or message and I will come and look at the spot with you.
