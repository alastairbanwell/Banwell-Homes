About Banwell Homes

Hands-on. Independent. Since 2014.

A strong background in the building industry, twelve-plus years of independent carpentry and fencing work, brought to every project in Betty's Bay and the wider Overberg.

The Story

Built on real experience.

Neville Banwell has worked as an independent carpenter and fencing specialist across the Overberg region since September 2014. Every project, from a full deck rebuild to a section of dropper fencing, is personally overseen by Neville from first site visit to final cleanup: measurements, client consultation, material sourcing, cost estimation, and the build itself.

He works with a small, trusted crew, usually two or three guys, never a distant subcontractor. There's no gap between who quotes the job and who's actually on site building it, backed by twelve-plus years of hands-on timber work.
