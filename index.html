The Story

Twelve-Plus Years
of hands-on work.

Neville Banwell has been building and restoring timber structures across the Overberg since 2014, hands-on, and based right here in Betty's Bay. From hardwood decks and pergolas to custom staircases and fencing, every project is personally overseen by Neville, from first site visit to final cleanup, with a small trusted crew helping bring it to life.

That background shows in the work: real knowledge of timber selection, coastal weathering protection, and the structural fasteners that actually hold up against the salt air on this coast, not a generic build.
